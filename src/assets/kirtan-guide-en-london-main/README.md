# Kīrtan Guide

**Twelfth Edition**

Intended as a useful guide to kīrtans chanted at **Śrī Chaitanya Sāraswat Maṭh**, **Nabadwip**, as established by\
the Founder President Āchārya **Śrīla Bhakti Rakṣak Śrīdhar Dev-Goswāmī Mahārāj**\
and his Successor President Āchārya **Śrīla Bhakti Sundar Govinda Dev-Goswāmī Mahārāj**.

With standardised transliteration and additional selected kīrtans from the Maṭh’s original Bengali _Śrī Gauḍīya-Gītāñjali_.
