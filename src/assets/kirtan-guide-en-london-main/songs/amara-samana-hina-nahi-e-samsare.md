# Āmāra Samāna Hīna Nāhi

### from Kalyaṇa-kalpa-taru by Śrīla Bhakti Vinod Ṭhākur

#### 1

    āmāra samāna hīna nāhi e saṁsāre
    asthira hayechhi paḍi’ bhava-pārāvāre

There is no one as wretched as I am in this world. I have fallen into this ocean of material existence and become distraught.

#### 2

    kula-devī yogamāyā more kṛpā kari’
    āvaraṇa samvaribe kabe viśvodarī

O family Goddess! O Yogamāyā! When will you bestow your mercy upon me and remove the veil (of illusion) O You from whose womb manifests the universe?

#### 3

    śunechhi āgame vede mahimā tomāra
    śrī-kṛṣṇa-vimukhe bā̐dhi’ karāo saṁsāra

I have heard of your glories in the Āgamas and Vedas. You bind souls who are averse to Śrī Kṛṣṇa and subject them to saṁsāra.

#### 4

    śrī-kṛṣṇa-sāmmukhya yāra bhāgya-krame haya
    tāre mukti diyā kara aśoka abhaya

To those who by good fortune have become inclined to Śrī Kṛṣṇa, you grant liberation and make them free from lamentation and fear.

#### 5

    e dāse janani kari’ akaitava dayā
    vṛndāvane deha’ sthāna tumi yogamāyā

O Mother! Please bestow your non-deceiving grace upon this servant and give me a place in Vṛndāvan. You are Yogamāyā.

#### 6

    tomāke laṅghiyā kothā jīve kṛṣṇa pāya
    kṛṣṇa rāsa prakaṭila tomāra kṛpāya

How can a jīva who neglects you attain Kṛṣṇa? Kṛṣṇa manifested the Rāsa Dance by your mercy.

#### 7

    tumi kṛṣṇa-sahacharī jagata-jananī
    tumi dekhāile more kṛṣṇa-chintāmaṇi

You are Kṛṣṇa’s companion and the mother of the world. You have shown me Kṛṣṇa, the wish-fulfilling jewel.

#### 8

    niṣkapaṭa haye mātā chāo mora pāne
    vaiṣṇave viśvāsa vṛddhi haka prati-kṣaṇe

O mother, please non-deceivingly glance upon me. May my faith in the Vaiṣṇava increase at every moment.

#### 9

    vaiṣṇava-charaṇa vinā bhava-pārāvāra
    bhakati-vinoda nāre ha-ibāre pāra

Without (shelter at) the feet of the Vaiṣṇavas, Bhakti Vinod cannot reach the shore of the ocean of material existence.
