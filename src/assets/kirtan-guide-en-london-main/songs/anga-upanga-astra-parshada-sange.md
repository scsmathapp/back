# Aṅga-upāṅga-astra-pārṣada-saṅge

### from Gītāvalī by Śrīla Bhakti Vinod Ṭhākur

#### 1

    aṅga-upāṅga-astra-pārṣada-saṅge
    nācha-i bhāva-mūrati gorā raṅge

In the company of His expansions (Nityānanda Prabhu and Advaita Āchārya), sub-expansions (Śrīvās Ṭhākur and other devotees), weapon (Śrī Harināma), and associates (Gadādhar Paṇḍit and other intimate companions), Gorā, the embodiment of divine love, dances in ecstasy.

#### 2

    gāota kali-yuga-pāvana-nāma
    bhrama-i śachī-suta nadīyā-dhāma

Singing the Name which purifies the Age of Kali, the son of Śachī roams throughout the abode of Nadia.
