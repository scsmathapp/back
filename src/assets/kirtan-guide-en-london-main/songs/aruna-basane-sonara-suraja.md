# Śrī Śrī Gaurasundarer Āvirbhāva Vāsare

## The Holy Appearance Day of Śrī Śrī Gaurasundar

### by Śrīla Bhakti Rakṣak Śrīdhar Dev-Goswāmī Mahārāj

#### 1

    aruṇa vasane    sonāra sūraja
        udichhe kena re āja
    vasanta suṣamā    ujāri āpanā
        ḍhāle kena jaga-mājha

Why has the Lord appeared suddenly with golden complexion and with golden dress just like the morning sun? Why has He come in the middle of springtime as everything feels so beautiful and wonderful?

#### 2

    taru-gulma-latā    apūrva vāratā
        vahe kena phale phule
    bhṛṅga o vihaṅge    kena hena raṅge
        saṅgīta taraṅga tule

Trees, bushes, and creeping vines – all display a wondrous newness. By what cause do they bear such fruits and flowers? Why do the bees and birds in such delight raise waves of melodious song?

#### 3

    patita durjana    kena re garjana
        ullāse phāṭhiyā paḍe
    vidyā-kula-dhana-    abhimānī jana
        kena mlāna duḥkha bhare

I am very fallen and unqualified. Why is this sound vibration coming to me, and why am I getting inspiration through it? Those who have high caste, vast knowledge and material wealth, they have so much ego and pride – why are they suffering?

#### 4

    ākāśa-vātāsa    ghuchāiya trāsa
        āśvāse bhāsāye deya
    sādhu-jana-mana    sukha vitaraṇa-
        āveśe unmāda haya

The sky and the air are so beautiful in the spring season, and they are naturally nourishing and inspiring everybody. In the same way, the sound vibration of the devotees spreads everywhere automatically as they distribute Kṛṣṇa’s Name.

#### 5

    chaudikete dhvani    ki apūrva śuni
        bahu-jana-uchcharola
    hare kṛṣṇa rāma    nāma divya-dhāma
        hari hari hari bola

What a remarkable sound is heard in all directions bringing jubilation to all the people. The devotees chant the divine Names “Hare, Kṛṣṇa, Rāma”, and the transcendental abode manifests. Chant “Hari Hari Hari!”

#### 6

    phālgunī pūrṇimā    hindola raṅgimā
        sujana bhajana-rāge
    saṅkīrtana sane    marama gahane
        nā jāni ki bhāva jāge

On His holy appearance day, the festival of Holi takes place. All the devotees are chanting as well as all the Hindus. No one can understand the feeling and the substance that comes within the heart from that saṅkīrtan.

#### 7

    sandhyā samāgama    tapana magana
        kena hema ghana kole
    aparūpa kata    pūrava parvata
        suvarṇa chandramā bhāle

As the sunset approaches, the sun shines on the mountain illuminating it like it has golden ornaments. This is especially so in the springtime. Then as the moon rises, it also beautifies that mountain with its rays.

#### 8

    suvarṇa chandramā    paśichhe nīlimā
        se nīla vilīna heme
    ithe kibā bhāya    sādhu-jana gāya
        kalaṅka nā rahe preme

The blue sky is covered by the Golden Moon, and when the devotees glorify the Lord with their discussions, no other conception or motive can exist other than Kṛṣṇa consciousness. At that time, prema, divine love, descends automatically without any impediment.

#### 9

    mahājane bale    grahaṇera chhale
        saṅge nāma-saṅkīrtana
    gaurachandrodaya    pāpa rāhu kṣaya
        chandra-śobhā-prema-dhana

The great devotees say, take up your spiritual path with the congregational chanting of the Lord’s Holy Names. With the appearance of Śrī Gaurachandra, all sinfulness is removed and everyone attains the treasure of love of Godhead.

#### 10

    marmajña sakale    kahe kutūhale
        nīlimā vilīna chā̐de
    chhanna avatāra    lukāna kāhāra?
        rādhā-ruchi-rūpa-chhā̐de

Everyone who engages in this Nām-saṅkīrtan of Mahāprabhu comes to enquire sincerely and to know the innermost secret of the Lord’s bluish colour absorbed by His golden features. This is the hidden incarnation of the Lord and the concealed feature of His form is that He is tasting the threefold relish of Śrī Rādhā.

#### 11

    ithe hena stuti    rādhā-bhāva-dyuti
        suvalita śyāma rāo
    udila gaurāṅga    nāma-prema-saṅga
        jaya jaya gorā gāo

Thus I pray to the dark cowherd boy Śyāmasundar who has taken the mood and effulgence of Śrī Rādhā. That Lord, Śrī Chaitanya, appeared with the chanting of the pure divine Name; everyone chant the glories of that Golden Lord, Śrī Chaitanya Mahāprabhu.
