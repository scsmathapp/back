# Śrī Śrī Upadeśāmṛta

## Verse Two

### by Śrīla Rūpa Goswāmī Prabhu in Sanskrit

    atyāhāraḥ prayāsaś cha prajalpo niyamāgrahaḥ
    jana-saṅgaś cha laulyañ cha ṣaḍbhir bhaktir vinaśyati

One’s devotional service is spoiled when one becomes too entangled in the following six activities: (1) eating more than necessary or collecting more funds than required; (2) over-endeavouring for mundane things that are very difficult to obtain; (3) talking unnecessarily about mundane subject matters; (4) practising the scriptural rules and regulations only for the sake of following them and not for the sake of spiritual advancement, or rejecting the rules and regulations of the scriptures and working independently or whimsically; (5) associating with worldly-minded persons who are not interested in Kṛṣṇa con­sciousness; and (6) being greedy for mundane achievements.

*Śrīla Bhakti Vinod Ṭhākur’s corresponding Bengali composition:*

#### 1

    atyāhāra prayāsa prajalpa jana-saṅga
    laulyādi niyamāgraha hale bhakti-bhaṅga

When over-accumulation, over-endeavour, unnecessary talk, association with non-devotees, fickleness, and misapplication of rules occur, bhakti becomes obstructed.

#### 2

    gṛha-tyāgī janera sañchaya atyāhāra
    adhika sañchayī gṛhī vaiṣṇavera chhāra

For renunciants, any accumulation is excessive (atyāhāra), and for householder devotees, excessive accumulation is bad.

#### 3

    bhakti-anukūla naya se saba udyama
    prayāsa nāmete tāra prakāśa viṣama

All endeavours that are not favourable to bhakti are called prayāsa; occurrences of them are condemnable.

#### 4

    grāmya-kathā prajalpa nāmete parichaya
    matera chāñchalya laulya asat-tṛṣṇāmaya

Village talk is called prajalpa. Restlessness of thought produced by material desire is called laulya.

#### 5-6

    viṣayī yoṣit-saṅgī tat-tat-saṅgī āra
    māyāvādī dharma-dhvajī nāstika prakāra

    se saba asat-saṅga bhakti-hānikara
    viśeṣa yatane sei saṅga parihara

Materialists, those who are attached to women, their companions, māyāvādīs, religious hypocrites, and atheists – the association of such persons is destructive to bhakti. With great care, avoid all such association.

#### 7

    niyama agraha āra niyama āgraha
    dvi-prakāra doṣa ei bhakta-galagraha

The faults of both being negligent of rules and being obsessive about rules are burdens to devotees.

#### 8

    eke svādhikāragata niyama varjana
    āre anya-adhikāra niyama grahana

In one, there is rejection of the rules meant for one’s own stage, and in the other, there is adherence to the rules meant for stages other than one’s own.
