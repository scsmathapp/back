# Śrī Śrī Upadeśāmṛta

## Verse Seven

### by Śrīla Rūpa Goswāmī Prabhu in Sanskrit

    syāt kṛṣṇa-nāma-charitādi-sitāpy avidyā-
    pittopatapta-rasanasya na rochikā nu
    kintv ādarād anudinaṁ khalu saiva juṣṭā
    svādvī kramād bhavati tad-gada-mūla-hantrī

The Holy Name, Character, Pastimes and Activities of Kṛṣṇa are all transcendentally sweet like sugar candy. Although the tongue of one afflicted by the jaundice of avidyā (ignorance) cannot taste anything sweet, it is wonderful that simply by carefully chanting these sweet Names every day, a natural relish awakens within one’s tongue, and one’s disease is gradually destroyed at the root.

*Śrīla Bhakti Vinod Ṭhākur’s corresponding Bengali composition:*

#### 1

    avidyā pittera doṣe duṣṭa rasanāya
    kṛṣṇa-saṅkīrtane ruchi nāhi haya hāya

There is no taste for chanting about Kṛṣṇa on a tongue that is defiled by the jaundice of ignorance. Alas!

#### 2

    sitapala prāya kṛṣṇa-kathā anudina
    ādare sevite ruchi dena samīchīna

Like sugar candy, daily engaging in discussion of Kṛṣṇa with faith produces genuine ruchi.

#### 3

    kṛṣṇa-kāmya-vismṛti avidyā-gada-mūla
    kṛṣṇa-saṅkīrtana-krame haya ta’ nirmūla

The root of the disease – ignorance, forgetfulness that Kṛṣṇa is our desired end – is gradually uprooted by chanting about Kṛṣṇa.

#### 4

    sei krame kṛṣṇa-nāmādite āsvādana
    anudina bāḍe, ruchi haya anukṣaṇa

By chanting, the taste of Kṛṣṇa’s Name, Pastimes, and so on, increases day by day, and one’s ruchi becomes constant.
