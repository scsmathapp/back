# Nīlāchale Prasāda-sevana

### by Śrīla Bhakti Vinod Ṭhākur

#### 1

    bhāi re!
    eka-dina nīlāchale    prasāda-sevana-kāle
        mahāprabhu śrī-kṛṣṇa-chaitanya
    balilena bhakta-gaṇe,    “khecharānna śuddha-mane
        sevā kari’ hao āja dhanya

O brother! One day in Nīlāchal at the time of honouring prasādam, Mahāprabhu Śrī Kṛṣṇa Chaitanya said to the devotees, “Be fortunate today by honouring khichuḍi with pure hearts.

#### 2

    “khecharānna piṭhā-pānā    apūrva prasāda nānā
        jagannātha dila tomā sabe
    ākaṇṭha bhojana kari’    bala mukhe hari hari
        avidyā-durita nāhi rabe

“Lord Jagannātha has given you khichuḍi, pancakes, sherbet, and various other types of wonderful prasād. Eat up to your neck and chant the Name of Hari! Ignorance and sin will disappear.

#### 3

    “jagannātha-prasādānna    viriñchi-śambhura mānya
        khāile prema ha-ibe udaya
    emana durlabha dhana    pāiyāchha sarva-jana
        jaya jaya jagannātha jaya”

“Lord Jagannātha’s prasād is honoured by Lord Brahmā and Lord Śiva. If you eat it, divine love will arise within you. You all have now attained such a precious treasure. Glory! Glory! Glory to Lord Jagannātha!”
