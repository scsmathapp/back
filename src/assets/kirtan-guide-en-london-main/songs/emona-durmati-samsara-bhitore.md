# Dainyātmikā

### by Śrīla Bhakti Vinod Ṭhākur

#### 1

    prabhu he!
    emana durmati    saṁsāra bhitare
        paḍiyā āchhinu āmi
    tava nija-jana    kona mahājane
        pāṭhāiyā dile tumi

O Lord! A wicked mind brought me into this world, but one of Your pure and elevated devotees has come to bring me out.

#### 2

    dayā kari’ more    patita dekhiyā
        kahila āmāre giyā
    ohe dīna-jana    śuna bhāla kathā
        ullasita habe hiyā

He saw me so fallen and wretched, took pity, and came to me saying, “O humbled soul, please listen to this good tiding, for it will gladden your heart.

#### 3

    tomāre tārite    śrī-kṛṣṇa-chaitanya
        navadvīpe avatāra
    tomā hena kata    dīna-hīna jane
        karilena bhava-pāra

“Śrī Kṛṣṇa Chaitanya has appeared in the land of Nabadwīp to deliver you. He has safely conducted many miserable souls such as you across the sea of worldly existence.

#### 4

    vedera pratijñā    rākhibāra tare
        rukma-varṇa vipra-suta
    mahāprabhu nāme    nadīyā mātāya
        saṅge bhāi avadhūta

“To fulfil the promise of the Vedas, the son of a brāhmaṇa, bearing the name Mahāprabhu, of golden complexion, has descended with His brother, the avadhūt Nityānanda. Together They have overwhelmed all of Nadia with divine ecstasy.

#### 5

    nanda-suta yini    chaitanya gosāñī
        nija nāma kari’ dāna
    tārila jagat    tumio yāiyā
        laha nija paritrāṇa

“Śrī Chaitanya, who is Kṛṣṇa Himself, the son of Nanda, has saved the world by freely distributing His own Holy Name. Go also and receive your deliverance.”

#### 6

    se kathā śuniyā    āsiyāchhi, nātha!
        tomāra charaṇa-tale
    bhakati-vinoda    kā̐diyā kā̐diyā
        āpana-kāhinī bale

O Lord, hearing those words, Bhakti Vinod has come weeping to the soles of Your lotus feet and tells the story of his life.
