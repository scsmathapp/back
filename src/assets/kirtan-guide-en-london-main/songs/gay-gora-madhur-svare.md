# Śrī Nāma

### by Śrīla Bhakti Vinod Ṭhākur

#### 1

    gāy gorā madhur svare
    “hare kṛṣṇa hare kṛṣṇa kṛṣṇa kṛṣṇa hare hare
    hare rāma hare rāma rāma rāma hare hare”

Lord Gaurasundar sings in a very sweet voice, “Hare Kṛṣṇa Hare Kṛṣṇa, Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa Hare Hare, Hare Rāma Hare Rāma, Rāma Rāma Hare Hare.”

#### 2

    gṛhe thāka, vane thāka, sadā ‘hari’ bale’ ḍāka
    sukhe duḥkhe bhulo nāka, vadane hari-nām kara re

Whether you live at home or in the forest, always chant the Lord’s Holy Name; in happiness or sadness, don’t forget – chant the Holy Name and fill your lips with Harināma.

#### 3

    māyā-jāle baddha haye, āchha michhe kāja laye
    ekhan-o chetana peye, rādhā-mādhav-nām bala re

Bound in māyā’s net, you’ve slaved and toiled in vain. But now that you have a human life and consciousness, chant the Lord’s Name, “Rādhā-Mādhava.”

#### 4

    jīvana ha-ila śeṣa, nā bhajile hṛṣīkeśa
    bhakti-vinod-upadeśa, ek-bār nām-rase māta re

This life must surely end soon, and you have not worshipped Hṛṣīkeśa, the master of the senses. Bhakti Vinod advises: at least once, taste the Nāma-rasa, the nectar of the Holy Name.
