# Nivedana

## Part Three

### by Śrīla Bhakti Vinod Ṭhākur

#### 1

        gopīnāth, āmāra upāya nāi
    tumi kṛpā kari’    āmāre la-ile
        saṁsāre uddhāra pāi

O Gopīnāth, I have no means of success, but if You take me, having bestowed Your mercy upon me, then I will obtain deliverance from this world.

#### 2

        gopīnāth, paḍechhi māyāra phere
    dhana, dārā, suta    ghirechhe āmāre
        kāmete rekhechhe jere

O Gopīnāth, I have fallen into the perils of material illusion. Wealth, wife, and sons have surrounded me, and lust has wasted me away.

#### 3

        gopīnāth, mana ye pāgala mora
    nā māne śāsana    sadā achetana
        viṣaye rayechhe bhora

O Gopīnāth, my mind is crazy and does not care for any authority. It is always senseless and has remained in the dark pit of worldly affairs.

#### 4

        gopīnāth, hāra ye menechhi āmi
    aneka yatana    ha-ila viphala
        ekhana bharasā tumi

O Gopīnāth, I have accepted my defeat. All of my various endeavours were useless. Now You are the only hope.

#### 5

        gopīnāth, kemane ha-ibe gati
    prabala indriya    vaśī-bhūta mana
        nā chhāḍe viṣaya-rati

O Gopīnāth, how shall I make any advancement when my mind has come under the control of the powerful senses and does not abandon its attachment to materialism?

#### 6

        gopīnāth, hṛdaye vasiyā mora
    manake śamiyā    laha nija-pāne
        ghuchibe vipada ghora

O Gopīnāth, after sitting down in the core of my heart and subduing my mind, please take me to You. In this way the horrible dangers of this world will disappear.

#### 7

        gopīnāth, anātha dekhiyā more
    tumi hṛṣīkeśa    hṛṣīka damiyā
        tāra’ he saṁsṛti-ghore

O Gopīnāth, You are Hṛṣīkeśa, the Lord of the senses. Seeing me so helpless, please control these senses of mine and deliver me from this dark and perilous worldly existence.

#### 8

        gopīnāth, galāya legechhe phā̐sa
    kṛpā-asi dhari’    bandhana chhediyā
        vinode karaha dāsa

O Gopīnāth, the noose of materialism has become fixed around my neck. Taking up the sword of Your mercy and cutting this bondage, make this Bhakti Vinod Your humble servant.

    (he govinda! gopīnātha!)
    (ei bār āmāy dayā kara he govinda! gopīnātha!)
    (bahu janme aparādhī he govinda! gopīnātha!)
    (lakṣa lakṣa janam gela he govinda! gopīnātha!)
    (śrī-charaṇe śaraṇ nilām he govinda! gopīnātha!)
    (śrī-charaṇe sevā diyā he govinda! gopīnātha!)

(O Govinda! O Gopīnāth!)\
(This time, please be merciful to me, O Govinda! O Gopīnāth!)\
(I have been an offender for many births, O Govinda! O Gopīnāth!)\
(Millions of births have passed, O Govinda! O Gopīnāth!)\
(I have taken shelter at Your holy feet, O Govinda! O Gopīnāth!)\
(I have surrendered at Your feet, O Govinda! O Gopīnāth!)\
(Please give me the service of Your feet, O Govinda! O Gopīnāth!)
