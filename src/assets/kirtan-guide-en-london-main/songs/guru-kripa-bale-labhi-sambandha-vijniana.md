# Śrī Śrī Upadeśāmṛta

## Sweet Upward Guidance to Eternity
## Verse One

### Eleven Sanskrit verses by Śrīla Rūpa Goswāmī Prabhu

    vācho vegaṁ manasaḥ krodha-vegaṁ
    jihvā-vegam udaropastha-vegam
    etān vegān yo viṣaheta dhīraḥ
    sarvām apīmāṁ pṛthivīṁ sa śiṣyāt

A sober person who can tolerate (1) the urge to speak, (2) the mind’s demands, (3) the actions of anger, and the urges of (4) the tongue, (5) the belly, and (6) the genitals, is qualified to instruct the entire world.

*Śrīla Bhakti Vinod Ṭhākur’s corresponding Bengali composition:*

#### 1

    guru-kṛpā-bale labhi’ sambandha-vijñāna
    kṛti-jīva hayena bhajane yatnavān

Attaining proper sambandha-jñān by the mercy of Śrī Guru, fortunate jīvas engage in bhajan.

#### 2

    sei jīve śrī-rūpa-gosvāmi mahodaya
    ‘upadeśāmṛte’ dhanya karena niśchaya

Śrīla Rūpa Goswāmī has certainly blessed such jīvas with *Śrī Upadeśāmṛta*.

#### 3

    gṛhī gṛha-tyāgī bhede dvi-prakāra jane
    upadeśa bheda vichāribe vijña-gaṇe

There are two types of practitioners – householders and renunciants, and the wise will conclude that there are different instructions for each of them.

#### 4

    gṛhī prati ei saba upadeśa haya
    gṛha-tyāgī prati ihā parākāṣṭhāmaya

All these instructions are for householders, and they are of utmost importance for renunciants.

#### 5-6

    vākya-vega mano-vega krodha-vega āra
    jihva-vega udara-upastha-vega chhāra

    ei chhaya vega sahi’ kṛṣṇa-nāmāśraye
    jagat śāsite pāre parājiyā bhaye

Those who tolerate the six base urges – the urges of speech, the mind, anger, the tongue, the belly, and the genitals – by taking the shelter of Kṛṣṇa’s Name can instruct the world and conquer fear.

#### 7

    kevala śaraṇāgati kṛṣṇa-bhaktimaya
    bhakti-pratikūla tyāga tāra aṅga haya

Pure surrender is made up of Kṛṣṇa-bhakti. Rejecting that which is unfavourable to bhakti is one of its limbs.

#### 8

    chhaya vega sahi’ yukta-vairāgya-āśraye
    nāme aparādha-śūnya ha-ibe nirbhaye

Those who tolerate these six urges in the shelter of yukta-vairāgya will fearlessly become free from the offences to the Holy Name.
