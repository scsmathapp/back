# Gurudeva! Kabe Mora Sei Dina Habe

### by Śrīla Bhakti Vinod Ṭhākur

#### 1

    gurudeva! kabe mora sei dina habe
    mana sthira kari’    nirjane basiyā
        kṛṣṇa-nāma gāba yabe
    saṁsāra-phukāra    kāṇe nā paśibe
        deha-roga dūre rabe

O Gurudev! When will that day be mine, when I will steady my mind and, sitting in a solitary place, chant Kṛṣṇa’s Name? Then the caco­phony of material existence will no longer enter my ears, and the disease of the body will remain far away.

#### 2

    ‘hare kṛṣṇa’ bali’    gāhite gāhite
        nayane vahibe lora
    dehete pulaka    udita ha-ibe
        premete karibe bhora

Absorbed in constantly calling out, “Hare Kṛṣṇa” as I chant, tears will flow from my eyes, horripilation will appear on my body, and I will become overwhelmed with prema.

#### 3

    gada-gada vāṇī    mukhe vāhiribe
        kā̐pibe śarīra mama
    gharma muhur-muhuḥ    vivarṇa ha-ibe
        stambhita pralaya-sama

Faltering speech will come from my mouth. My body will tremble, perspire again and again, turn pale, and become stunned as though devastated.

#### 4

    niṣkapaṭe hena    daśā kabe habe
        nirantara nāma gāba
    āveśe rahiyā    deha-yātrā kari’
        tomāra karuṇā pāba

When will I genuinely reach such a state? Then I will chant the Name continuously. Remaining in ecstasy as I maintain the body, I will attain your mercy.
