# Gurudeva! Kabe Tava Karuṇā-prakāśe

### by Śrīla Bhakti Vinod Ṭhākur

#### 1

    gurudeva! kabe tava karuṇā-prakāśe
    śrī-gaurāṅga-līlā    haya nitya-tattva
        ei dṛḍha viśvāse
    ‘hari’ ‘hari’ bali’    godruma-kānane
        bhramiba darśana-āśe

O Gurudev! By a manifestation of your mercy and with firm faith that Śrī Gaurāṅga’s līlā is an eternal reality, when will I wander amidst the forest of Godruma calling out, “Hari!” “Hari!” with the hope of darśan?

#### 2

    nitāi, gaurāṅga,    advaita, śrīvāsa,
        gadādhara pañcha-jana
    kṛṣṇa-nāma-rase    bhāsābe jagat
        kari’ mahā-saṅkīrtana

Nitāi, Gaurāṅga, Advaita, Śrīvās, and Gadādhar – these five will inundate the world with the rasa of Kṛṣṇa’s Name by performing grand saṅkīrtan.

#### 3

    nartana-vilāsa    mṛdaṅga-vādana
        śuniba āpana-kāṇe
    dekhiyā dekhiyā    se līlā-mādhurī
        bhāsiba premera vāne

I will hear the sounds of the mṛdaṅgas and the play of Their dancing with my own ears. Beholding the beauty of this līlā, I will float in a flood of prema.

#### 4

    nā dekhi’ ābāra    se līlā-ratana
        kā̐di ‘hā gaurāṅga!’ bali’
    āmāre viṣayī    ‘pāgala’ baliyā
        aṅgete dibeka dhūli

Again, not seeing those jewel-like Pastimes before me, I cry, calling out, “O Gaurāṅga!” Considering me mad, the worldly will kick dust on my body.
