# Gurudeva!

### by Śrīla Bhakti Vinod Ṭhākur

#### 1

    gurudeva!
    kṛpā-bindu diyā    kara’ ei dāse
        tṛṇāpekṣā ati dīna
    sakala-sahane    bala diyā kara
        nija-māne spṛhā-hīna

Gurudev, please give to this servant just one drop of mercy, and make me more humble than a blade of grass. Grant me the strength to endure all things, and make me free from personal honour.

#### 2

    sakale sammāna    karite śakati
        deha’ nātha! yathā yatha
    tabe ta’ gāiba    hari-nāma sukhe
        aparādha habe hata

Give me energy so that I can properly give respect and honour to all. Then I shall be able to happily chant the Holy Name, and my offences will go away.

#### 3

    kabe hena kṛpā    labhiyā e jana
        kṛtārtha ha-ibe nātha!
    śakti-buddhi-hīna    āmi ati dīna
        kara more ātma-sātha

O my Lord! When will such mercy fall to this person. I am weak, bereft, and devoid of intelligence. Please allow me to be with you.

#### 4

    yogyatā-vichāre    kichhu nāhi pāi
        tomāra karuṇā sāra
    karuṇā nā haile    kā̐diyā kā̐diyā
        prāṇa nā rākhiba āra

If you examine me, you will find no qualities. Your mercy is everything to me. If you are not merciful unto me, I can only weep, and I will not be able to maintain my life.
