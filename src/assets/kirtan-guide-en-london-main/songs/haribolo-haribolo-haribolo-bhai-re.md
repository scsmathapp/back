# Haribala Bhāi Re

### by Śrīla Bhakti Vinod Ṭhākur

#### 1

    haribala haribala haribala bhāi re
    hari-nāma āniyāchhe gauraṅga-nitāi re
        (modera duḥkha dekhe re)

O brother! Chant “Hari”! Chant “Hari”! Chant “Hari”! Gaurāṅga and Nitāi have brought Hari’s Name! (Seeing our suffering!)

#### 2

    hari-nāma vinā jīvera anya dhana nāi re
    hari-nāme śuddha hala jagāi-mādhāi re
        (baḍa pāpī chhila re)

The jīva has no wealth other than Hari’s Name! Jagāi and Mādhāi were purified by Hari’s Name! (They were great sinners!)

#### 3

    michhe māyā-baddha haye jīvana kāṭāi re
        (āmi āmāra bale re)
    āśā-vaśe ghure’ ghure’ āra kothā yāi re
        (āśāra śeṣa nāi re)

Bound by Māyā, I pass my life in vain! (Claiming ‘I’ and ‘mine’!) Controlled by desire, wandering and wandering, where else will I go? (Desire has no end!)

#### 4

    ‘hari-bale’ deo bhāi āśāra mukhe chhāi re
        (nirāśa ta’ sukha re)
    bhoga-mokṣa-vāñchhā chhāḍi’ hari-nāma gāi re
        (śuddha-sattva haye re)

Brother! Chanting “Hari”, throw ash in the face of desire! (Desireless­ness is happiness!) Giving up desire for enjoyment and liberation, I will chant Hari’s Name! (Being pure in heart!)

#### 5

    nā cheyeo nāmera guṇe o saba phala pāi re
        (tuchchha phaler prayāsa chheḍe re)
    vinoda bale yāi laye nāmera bālāi re
        (nāmera bālāi chheḍe re)

Even without desiring them, I obtain all those ends (dharma, artha, kāma, and mokṣa) by the power of Hari’s Name! (Give up endeavours for insignificant ends!) Vinod says, “I will remove the obstructions to Hari’s Name! (Give up the obstructions to Hari’s Name!)”
