# Śrī Śrī Guru Gaurāṅga Rādhā Madana-Mohanjīu

## Kolkata, Śrī Chaitanya Sāraswata Kṛṣṇānuśīlana Sangha

    jayatāṁ suratau paṅgor mama manda-mater gatī
    mat-sarvasva-padāmbhojau rādhā-madana-mohanau

*(Śrī Chaitanya-charitāmṛta: Ādi-līlā, 1.15)*

O Śrī Śrī Rādhā-Madana-mohan, I am a helpless cripple and my meagre intelligence is absorbed in vile material sense objects. Kindly let deep devotional attachment arise for the exclusive wealth of my life which is Your lotus feet. May Your Lordships, who are so compassionate and affectionate, be forever victorious!

*(Śrīla Kṛṣṇadās Kavirāj Goswāmī)*
