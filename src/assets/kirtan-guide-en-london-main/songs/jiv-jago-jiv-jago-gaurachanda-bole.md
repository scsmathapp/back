# Aruṇodaya-kīrtan

## Part Two

### by Śrīla Bhakti Vinod Ṭhākur

#### 1

    jīva jāga, jīva jāga, gorāchā̐da bale
    kata nidrā yāo māyā-piśāchīra kole

Lord Gaurāṅga calls, “Wake up, sleeping souls! Wake up, sleeping souls! You have slept so long in the lap of the witch Māyā.

    (ār kata kāl ghumāibe?)
    (māyā-piśāchīra kole ār kata kāl ghumāibe?)
    (uṭha jīva, kṛṣṇa bhaja ār kata kāl ghumāibe?)

(“How much longer will you sleep? How much longer will you sleep in the lap of the witch Māyā? Arise soul! Serve Kṛṣṇa! How much longer will you sleep?)

#### 2

    bhajiba baliyā ese saṁsāra-bhitare
    bhuliyā rahile tumi avidyāra bhare

“You came into this world saying, ‘O my Lord, I will certainly worship You’, but having forgotten this promise, you have remained in great ignorance.

    (bhuliyā rahile)
    (avidyāra mohe paḍe tumi bhuliyā rahile)

(“Having forgotten, you have remained there. You have fallen into the bewilderment of ignorance, and, having forgotten your promise, you have remained there.)

#### 3

    tomāre la-ite āmi ha-inu avatāra
    āmi vinā bandhu āra ke āchhe tomāra

“I have descended just to save you. Other than Myself, who else is your friend?

    (eman bandhu ār ke āchhe?)
    (māyā ha-ite uddhārite eman bandhu ār ke āchhe?)

(“Who else is such a friend? Who else is such a friend to rescue you from Māyā?)

#### 4

    enechhi auṣadhi māyā nāśibāra lāgi’
    hari-nāma mahāmantra lao tumi māgi’

“I have brought the medicine for destroying the illusion of māyā. Now pray for this Hari-nāma Mahāmantra and take it.”

    (māgiyā lao re)
    (sudhā-mākhā ei hari-nāma māgiyā lao re)

(“O pray and take it. O pray and take this Name of Hari which is coated with nectar.”)

#### 5

    bhakati-vinoda prabhura charaṇe paḍiyā
    sei hari-nāma mantra la-ila māgiyā

Ṭhākur Bhakti Vinod fell at the lotus feet of Lord Gaurāṅga, and after begging for the Holy Name he received that Mahāmantra.
