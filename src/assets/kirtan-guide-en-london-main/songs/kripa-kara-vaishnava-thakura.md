# Prārthanā Lālasāmayī

### by Śrīla Bhakti Vinod Ṭhākur

#### 1

    kṛpa kara vaiṣṇava ṭhākura
    sambandha jāniyā,    bhajite bhajite,
        abhimāna hau dūra

Please give your mercy to me now, oh revered pure devotee, Vaiṣṇava Ṭhākur! Then only, my false ego will go far away by my constant worship in full knowledge of my real eternal position.

#### 2

    ‘ami ta vaiṣṇava’    e buddhi haile,
        amānī nā ha’ba āmi
    pratiṣṭhāśā āsi’,    hṛdaya dūṣibe,
        haiba nirayagāmī

If I falsely think that ‘I am a Vaiṣṇava’, then I shall look forward to receiving respect from others. And if the desire for fame and reputation pollutes my heart, then certainly I shall go to live in hell instantly.

#### 3

    tomāra kiṅkara,    āpane jāniba,
        ‘guru’-abhimāna tyaji’
    tomāra uchchhiṣṭha,    padajala-reṇu,
        sadā niṣkapaṭe bhaji

Renouncing the false conception that I am a ‘guru’, I will understand myself to be your humble servant. I sincerely worship the remnants of your food, oh pure devotee, as well as the water that has washed the sacred dust of your lotus feet.

#### 4

    ‘nije sreṣṭha’ jāni’,    uchchhiṣṭhādi dāne,
        habe abhimāna bhāra
    tāi śiṣya tava,    thākiyā sarvadā,
        nā laiba pūjā kā’ra

By giving others the remnants of my food, I shall consider myself superior and shall be burdened with the weight of false pride. Therefore, always remaining your surrendered disciple, I shall not accept worship from anyone.

#### 5

    amānī mānada,    haile kīrtane
        adhikāra dibe tumi
    tomāra charaṇe,    niṣkapaṭe āmi,
        kā̐diyā luṭiba bhūmi

Please bestow upon me the qualities of not desiring honour, of giving honour to others, and thus the capacity to chant the Holy Name. Then, at your feet, I will sincerely weep in ecstasy while rolling on the ground.
