# Mā Muñcha Pañcha-daśakam

## Originally published in Śrī Gauḍīya Darśan, 12 January 1967

### by Śrīla Bhakti Rakṣak Śrīdhar Dev-Goswāmī Mahārāj.

#### 1

    mā muñcha muñcha māṁ kṛṣṇa! dāsaṁ dīna-dayārṇava!
    patitaṁ ghora-saṁsāre hy avaśaṁ prakṛter vaśat

I have fallen into this terrible world and am helplessly subjugated by material nature. O Kṛṣṇa! O Ocean of Mercy for the distressed! Don’t abandon me. Deliver this servant.

#### 2

    mā muñcha muñcha māṁ kṛṣṇa! dāsaṁ dīna-dayārṇava!
    anādi-bhāgya-vaiguṇyāt bhoga-mugdhaṁ durāśayam

Due to my ‘time-less’ misfortune, I have been bewildered by the wicked desire to selfishly enjoy. O Kṛṣṇa! O Ocean of Mercy for the distressed! Don’t abandon me. Deliver this servant.

#### 3

    mā muñcha muñcha māṁ kṛṣṇa! dāsaṁ dīna-dayārṇava!
    māyā-krīḍanakaṁ dīnaṁ nirālambaṁ nirāśrayam

I am a toy of Māyā Devī. I am distressed. I have no support. I have no shelter. O Kṛṣṇa! O Ocean of Mercy for the distressed! Don’t abandon me. Deliver this servant.

#### 4

    mā muñcha muñcha māṁ kṛṣṇa! dāsaṁ dīna-dayārṇava!
    janma-mṛtyu-jarā-vyādhi-bhīti-chintāti-kātaram

I am deeply troubled by birth, death, old age, disease, fear, and anxiety. O Kṛṣṇa! O Ocean of Mercy for the distressed! Don’t abandon me. Deliver this servant.

#### 5

    mā muñcha muñcha māṁ kṛṣṇa! dāsaṁ dīna-dayārṇava!
    jñānājñāna-kṛtānanta-pāpa-bhoga-bhayākulam

I am overwhelmed with fear as I suffer the reactions for the unlimited sins I have knowingly and unknowingly committed. O Kṛṣṇa! O Ocean of Mercy for the distressed! Don’t abandon me. Deliver this servant.

#### 6

    mā muñcha muñcha māṁ kṛṣṇa! dāsaṁ dīna-dayārṇava!
    kāma-krodhādi-dasyubhir nirdayaṁ padamarditam

I have been trampled by the feet of the merciless bandits – lust, anger, and so on. O Kṛṣṇa! O Ocean of Mercy for the distressed! Don’t abandon me. Deliver this servant.

#### 7

    mā muñcha muñcha māṁ kṛṣṇa! dāsaṁ dīna-dayārṇava!
    antaḥśatru-viparyastaṁ punaḥ punar vilañchhitam

I am bewildered and harassed by internal enemies again and again. O Kṛṣṇa! O Ocean of Mercy for the distressed! Don’t abandon me. Deliver this servant.

#### 8

    mā muñcha muñcha māṁ kṛṣṇa! dāsaṁ dīna-dayārṇava!
    trāṇārthaṁ prārthyamāno ’pi na māṁ paśyati kaśchanaḥ

Although I am praying for deliverance, no one sees me. O Kṛṣṇa! O Ocean of Mercy for the distressed! Don’t abandon me. Deliver this servant.

#### 9

    mā muñcha muñcha māṁ kṛṣṇa! dāsaṁ dīna-dayārṇava!
    pitaro bāndhavā devā asamarthāḥ parāṅmukhāḥ

My ancestors, my friends, and the demigods are all incapable (of delivering me) and opposed to me. O Kṛṣṇa! O Ocean of Mercy for the distressed! Don’t abandon me. Deliver this servant.

#### 10

    mā muñcha muñcha māṁ kṛṣṇa! dāsaṁ dīna-dayārṇava!
    sva-para-bharasā-hīnaṁ nirupāyaṁ nirākṛtam

I am devoid of any hope in myself and in others. I have no means whatsoever (of deliverance), and I am utterly spurned. O Kṛṣṇa! O Ocean of Mercy for the distressed! Don’t abandon me. Deliver this servant.

#### 11

    mā muñcha muñcha māṁ kṛṣṇa! dāsaṁ dīna-dayārṇava!
    mahāparādha-rāśi-nāmālayaṁ tyakta-sādhanam

I am an abode of a profusion of terrible offences against the Name, and I have abandoned the way of deliverance. O Kṛṣṇa! O Ocean of Mercy for the distressed! Don’t abandon me. Deliver this servant.

#### 12

    mā muñcha muñcha māṁ kṛṣṇa! dāsaṁ dīna-dayārṇava!
    he sad-asad-vichārorddha-prapannārtiharo hare!

O Lord! You remove the distress of the surrendered souls, who are beyond all consideration of good and bad. O Kṛṣṇa! O Ocean of Mercy for the distressed! Don’t abandon me. Deliver this servant.

#### 13

    mā muñcha muñcha māṁ kṛṣṇa! dāsaṁ dīna-dayārṇava!
    dāsatvaṁ dehi dāsānāṁ tava deva dayānidhe!

O Lord! O Ocean of Mercy! Please give me the service of Your servants. O Kṛṣṇa! O Ocean of Mercy for the distressed! Don’t abandon me. Deliver this servant.

#### 14

    mā muñcha muñcha māṁ kṛṣṇa! dāsaṁ dīna-dayārṇava!
    niyuñkṣva nityadāsye māṁ pradāya prema-vartanam

Please engage me in Your eternal service and grant me a salary of prema. O Kṛṣṇa! O Ocean of Mercy for the distressed! Don’t abandon me. Deliver this servant.

#### 15

    mā muñcha muñcha māṁ kṛṣṇa! dāsaṁ dīna-dayārṇava!
    svāntaraṅga-sevāṁ dehi he gopījana-vallabha!

Please bestow Your intimate service upon me, O Beloved of the gopīs! O Kṛṣṇa! O Ocean of Mercy for the distressed! Don’t abandon me. Deliver this servant.

#### 16

    mā muñcha-pañcha-daśakaṁ! kṛṣṇa dīna-dayārṇava!
    tridaṇḍi-śrīdharodgītaṁ gṛhānedaṁ stavāmṛtam

Tridaṇḍi Bhikṣu Śrī Śrīdhar sings aloud this Mā Muñcha Pañcha-Daśakam. O Ocean of Mercy for the distressed, Śrī Kṛṣṇa! Please accept this nectarean prayer.
