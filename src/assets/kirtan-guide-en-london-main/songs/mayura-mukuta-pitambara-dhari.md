# Mayūra-mukuṭa

### Sanskrit composition by Śrīla Bhakti Vinod Ṭhākur

#### 1

    (jaya) mayūra-mukuṭa-pītāmbara-dhārī
    muralī-dhara govardhana-dhārī

(All glories to) Śrī Kṛṣṇa, holder of the flute Muralī, and lifter of Govardhan Hill! O Kṛṣṇa, who wears a crown adorned with a pea­cock feather, and who is dressed in golden clothes!

#### 2

    (jaya) śrī-rādhā-mādhava kuñja-vihārī
    muralī-dhara govardhana-dhārī

O Kṛṣṇa, holder of the flute Muralī, and lifter of Govardhan Hill! O Kṛṣṇa, the Lord of Śrīmatī Rādhārāṇī, and who plays in the groves of Śrī Vṛndāvan!

#### 3

    (jaya) yaśodā-nandana kṛṣṇa murāri
    muralī-dhara govardhana-dhārī

O Kṛṣṇa, holder of the flute Muralī, and lifter of Govardhan Hill! All glories to You, Kṛṣṇa, son of Mother Yaśodā, and the killer of the Mura demon!

#### 4

    (jaya) gopī-jana-vallabha vaṁśī-vihārī
    muralī-dhara govardhana-dhārī

O Kṛṣṇa, holder of the flute Muralī, and lifter of Govardhan Hill! All glories to You, Kṛṣṇa, the dearmost lover of all the cowherd girls of Vṛndāvan, and the player of the flute Vaṁśī!
