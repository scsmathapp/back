# Śrīman Mahāprabhura Śata-nāma

## One Hundred Names of Śrīman Mahāprabhu

### by Śrīla Bhakti Vinod Ṭhākur

    nadīyā-nagare nitāi neche neche gāya re

Dancing and dancing throughout the town of Nadia, Nitāi sings!

#### 1

    jagannātha-suta mahāprabhu viśvambhara
    māyāpura-śaśi navadvīpa-sudhākara

(1) O son of Jagannātha Miśra, (2) O great master,\
(3) O maintainer of the world, (4) O moon of Māyāpur,\
(5) O source of nectar for Nabadwīp!

#### 2

    śachī-suta gaurahari nimāi-sundara
    rādhā-bhāva-kānti-āchchhādita naṭavara

(6) O son of Śachī, (7) O golden Hari, (8) O beautiful Nimāi,\
(9) O best of dancers covered with the heart and halo of Śrīmatī Rādhārāṇī!

#### 3

    nāmānanda chapala bālaka mātṛ-bhakta
    brahmāṇḍa-vadana tarkī kautukānurakta

(10) O He whose ecstasy is the Name, (11) O restless boy,\
(12) O devotee of His mother, (13) O He in whose mouth rests the entire universe, (14) O expert in logic, (15) O He who is fond of play!

#### 4

    vidyārthi-uḍupa chaura-dvayera mohana
    tairthika-sarvasva grāmya-bālikā-krīḍana

(16) O moon amongst the seekers of knowledge,\
(17) O bewilderer of the two theives, (18) O the all-in-all of pilgrims,\
(19) O He who plays with the village girls!

#### 5

    lakṣmī-prati vara-dātā uddhata bālaka
    śrī-śachīra pati-putra-śoka-nivāraka

(20) O giver of a boon to Lakṣmī Priyā, (21) O mischevious boy,\
(22) O remover of Śrī Śachī’s grief over her husband and son!

#### 6

    lakṣmī-pati pūrva-deśa-sarva-kleśa-hara
    digvijayi-darpa-hārī viṣṇu-priyeśvara

(23) O husband of Lakṣmī Priyā, (24) O remover of all suffering from East Bengal, (25) O remover of the pride of the champion scholar Keśava Kāśmīri, (26) O Lord of Viṣṇu Priyā!

#### 7

    ārya-dharma-pāla pitṛ-gayā-piṇḍa-dātā
    purī-śiṣya madhvāchārya-sampradāya-pātā

(27) O preserver of Āryan dharma, (28) O offerer of piṇḍa to His ancestors at Gayā, (29) O disciple of Śrī Īśvara Purī, (30) O protector of the sampradāya established by Śrī Madhva Āchārya!

#### 8

    kṛṣṇa-nāmonmatta kṛṣṇa-tattva-adhyāpaka
    nāma-saṅkīrtana-yuga-dharma-pravartaka

(31) O He who is maddened by the Name of Kṛṣṇa, (32) O teacher of the science of Kṛṣṇa, (33) O inaugurator of the dharma for the age: congregational chanting of the Holy Name!

#### 9

    advaita-bāndhava śrīnivāsa-gṛha-dhana
    nityānanda-prāṇa gadādharera jīvana

(34) O dear friend of Advaita, (35) O wealth of Śrīvās’ home,\
(36) O heart of Nityānanda, (37) O life of Gadādhar!

#### 10

    antardvīpa-śaśadhara sīmanta-vijaya
    godruma-vihārī madhyadvīpa-līlāśraya

(38) O moon of Antardwīp, (39) O triumph of Sīmantadwīp,\
(40) O reveller of Godrumadwīp,\
(41) O shelter of the Pastimes in Madhyadwīp!

#### 11

    koladvīpa-pati ṛtudvīpa-maheśvara
    jahnu-modadruma-rudradvīpera īśvara

(42) O Lord of Koladwīp, (43) O great Lord of Ṛtudwīp,\
(44–46) O Lord of Jahnudwīp, Modadrumadwīp, and Rudradwīp!

#### 12

    navakhaṇḍa-raṅganātha jāhnavī-jīvana
    jagāi-mādhāi-ādi-durvṛtta-tāraṇa

(47) O playful Lord of Nabadwīp, (48) O life of Gaṅgā,\
(49) O deliverer of Jagāi, Mādhāi, and other misbehaved persons!

#### 13

    nagara-kīrtana-siṁha kājī-uddhāraṇa
    śuddha-nāma-prachāraka bhaktārti-haraṇa

(50) O lion of the village kīrtans, (51) O saviour of the Kāzī,\
(52) O propagator of the pure Name,\
(53) O remover of the devotees’ distress!

#### 14

    nārāyaṇī-kṛpā-sindhu jīvera niyantā
    adhama-paḍuyā-daṇḍī bhakta-doṣa-hantā

(54) O ocean of mercy for Nārāyaṇī, (55) O controller of all souls,\
(56) O punisher of the degraded students,\
(57) O destroyer of the devotees’ faults!

#### 15

    śrī-kṛṣṇa-chaitanyachandra bhāratī-tāraṇa
    parivrāja-śiromaṇi utkala-pāvana

(58) O moon known as Śrī Kṛṣṇa Chaitanya, (59) O deliverer of Śrī Keśava Bhāratī, (60) O crown-jewel of the itinerants,\
(61) O purifier of Odisha!

#### 16

    ambuliṅga-bhuvaneśa-kapoteśa-pati
    kṣīra-chora-gopāla-darśana-sukhī yatī

(62–64) O Lord of Ambuliṅga, Bhuvaneśvara, and Kapoteśvara,\
(65) O He who is happy to see Kṣīra-chora Gopīnāth and Sākṣī Gopāl, (66) O ascetic!

#### 17

    nirdaṇḍi-sannyāsī sārvabhauma-kṛpāmaya
    svānanda-āsvādānandī sarva-sukhāśraya

(67) O sannyāsī without a daṇḍa, (68) O He who is merciful to Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭāchārya, (69) O He who is joyful in tasting the joy of Himself, (70) O origin of all happiness!

#### 18

    puraṭa-sundara vāsudeva-trāṇa-kartā
    rāmānanda-sakhā bhaṭṭa-kula-kleśa-hartā

(71) O beautiful golden Lord, (72) O deliverer of (the leper) Vāsudev,\
(73) O friend of Rāmānanda Rāy,\
(74) O remover of hardship from the Bhaṭṭa family!

#### 19

    bauddha-jaina-māyāvādi-kutarka-khaṇḍana
    dakṣiṇa-pāvana bhakti-grantha-uddhāraṇa

(75–77) O refuter of the fallacious arguments of the Buddhists, Jains, and Māyāvādīs, (78) O purifier of the South,\
(79) O recoverer of Śrī Brahma-saṁhitā and Śrī Kṛṣṇa-karṇāmṛta!

#### 20

    ālāla-darśanānandī rathāgra-nartaka
    gajapati-trāṇa devānanda-uddhāraka

(80) O He who is joyful to see Ālālanāth, (81) O dancer in front of the chariot, (82) O deliverer of Mahārāj Pratāparudra,\
(83) O saviour of Devānanda Paṇḍit!

#### 21

    kuliyā-prakāśe duṣṭa-paḍuyāra trāṇa
    rūpa-sanātana-bandhu sarva-jīva-prāṇa

(84) O deliverer of the impudent students during His appearance in Kuliyā, (85–86) O friend of Rūpa and Sanātan, (87) O life of all souls!

#### 22

    vṛndāvanānanda-mūrti balabhadra-saṅgī
    yavana-uddhārī bhaṭṭa-vallabhera raṅgī

(88) O embodiment of the joy of Vṛndāvan, (89) O companion of Balabhadra Bhaṭṭāchārya, (90) O deliverer of the foreigners,\
(91) O He who is jocular with Vallabha Bhaṭṭa!

#### 23

    kāśī-vāsi-sannyāsī-uddhārī prema-dātā
    markaṭa-vairāgī-daṇḍī āchaṇḍāla-trātā

(92) O saviour of the sannyāsīs of Kāśī, (93) O giver of divine love,\
(94) O punisher of the false monkey-like renunciants,\
(95) O deliverer of even the outcasts!

#### 24

    bhaktera gauravakārī bhakta-prāṇa-dhana
    haridāsa-raghunātha-svarūpa-jīvana

(96) O He who takes pride in the devotees, (97) O life and wealth of the devotees, (98–100) O life of Haridās, Raghunāth, and Svarūp!

#### 25

    nadiyā-nagare nitāi neche neche gāya re
    bhakati-vinoda tā̐ra paḍe rāṅgā-pāya re

Dancing and dancing throughout the town of Nadia, Nitāi sings, and Bhakti Vinod falls at His reddish feet.
