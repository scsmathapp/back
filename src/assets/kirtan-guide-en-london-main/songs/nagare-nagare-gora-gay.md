# Śrī Kṛṣṇera Viṁśottara-śata-nāma

## One Hundred and Twenty Names of Śrī Kṛṣṇa

### by Śrīla Bhakti Vinod Ṭhākur

    nagare nagare gorā gāya

From village to village, Śrī Gaurāṅga sings!

#### 1

    yaśomatī-stanya-pāyī śrī-nanda-nandana
    indra-nīla-maṇi vraja-janera jīvana

(1) O drinker of Mother Yaśodā’s breastmilk, (2) O son of Śrī Nanda,\
(3) O sapphire jewel, (4) O life of the people of Vraja!

#### 2

    śrī-gokula-niśācharī-pūtanā-ghātana
    duṣṭa-tṛṇāvarta-hantā śakaṭa-bhañjana

(5) O killer of the witch Pūtanā in Śrī Gokula,\
(6) O destroyer of the wicked Tṛṇāvarta, (7) O dispeller of Śakaṭa!

#### 3

    navanīta-chora dadhi-haraṇa-kuśala
    yamala-arjuna-bhañjī govinda gopāla

(8) O butter thief, (9) O expert at stealing yoghurt, (10) O breaker of the two Arjuna trees, (11) O pleaser of the cows, (12) O cowherd boy!

#### 4

    dāmodara vṛndāvana-go-vatsa-rākhāla
    vatsāsurāntaka hari nija-jana-pāla

(13) O He who is bound about the belly by a rope, (14) O keeper of the cows and calves of Vṛndāvan, (15) O destroyer of Vatsāsura,\
(16) O He who takes away (the sins, miseries, and obstacles to devotion from His devotees), (17) O maintainer of His own!

#### 5

    baka-śatru agha-hantā brahma-vimohana
    dhenuka-nāśana kṛṣṇa kāliya-damana

(18) O enemy of Baka, (19) O slayer of Agha, (20) O bewilderer of Brahmā, (21) O destroyer of Dhenuka, (22) O He who attracts and satisfies all, (23) O subduer of Kāliya!

#### 6

    pītāmbara śikhi-pichchha-dhārī veṇu-dhara
    bhāṇḍīra-kānana-līla dāvānala-hara

(24) O He of yellow garments, (25) O He who wears a peacock feather, (26) O holder of the veṇu flute, (27) O He who performs Pastimes in the Bhāṇḍīra forest, (28) O extinguisher of the forest fire!

#### 7

    naṭavara guhāchara śarata-vihārī
    vallabhī-vallabha deva gopī-vastra-hārī

(29) O best of dancers, (30) O He who wanders in the caves,\
(31) O reveller of the autumn, (32) O lover of the cowherd maidens,\
(33) O playful one, (34) O stealer of the gopīs’ clothes!

#### 8

    yajña-patnī-gaṇa-prati karuṇāra sindhu
    govardhana-dhṛk mādhava vraja-vāsī-bandhu

(35) O ocean of mercy for the wives of the brāhmaṇas engaged in sacrifice, (36) O lifter of Govardhan, (37) O descendent of Madhu,\
(38) O friends of the residents of Vraja!

#### 9

    indra-darpa-hārī nanda-rakṣitā mukunda
    śrī-gopī-vallabha rāsa-krīḍa pūrṇānanda

(39) O remover of Indra’s pride, (40) O protector of Nanda,\
(41) O giver of liberation, (42) O lover of the gopīs,\
(43) O player of Rāsa, (44) O complete joy!

#### 10

    śrī-rādhā-vallabha rādhā-mādhava sundara
    lalitā-viśākhā-ādi-sakhī-prāṇeśvara

(45) O lover of Śrī Rādhā, (46) O sweetness of Rādhā, (47) O beautiful one, (48) O Lord of the hearts of Lalitā, Viśākhā, and the other sakhīs!

#### 11

    nava-jaladhara-kānti madana-mohana
    vana-mālī smera-mukha gopī-prāṇa-dhana

(49) O He of the complexion of a fresh rain cloud,\
(50) O bewilderer of Cupid, (51) O He who wears a forest garland,\
(52) O He of smiling face, (53) O life and wealth of the gopīs!

#### 12

    tri-bhaṅgī muralī-dhara yāmuna-nāgara
    rādhā-kuṇḍa-raṅga-netā rasera sāgara

(54) O thrice bent, (55) O holder of the muralī flute, (56) O paramour on the Yamunā, (57) O leader of the play at Rādhā Kuṇḍa,\
(58) O ocean of rasa!

#### 13

    chandrāvalī-prāṇanātha kautukābhilāṣī
    rādhā-māna-sulampaṭa milana-prayāsī

(59) O Lord of the heart of Chandrāvalī, (60) O desirer for play,\
(61) O Lord, eager to overcome of Rādhā’s jealous anger,\
(62) O seeker of union!

#### 14

    mānasa-gaṅgāra dānī prasūna-taskara
    gopī-saha-haṭhakārī vraja-vaneśvara

(63) O tax-collector at Mānasa Gaṅgā, (64) O blossom thief, (65) O He who is impetuous with the gopīs, (66) O Lord of the forest of Vraja!

#### 15

    gokula-sampada gopa-duḥkha-nivāraṇa
    durmada-damana bhakta-santāpa-haraṇa

(67) O wealth of Gokula, (68) O vanquisher of the cowherders’ sorrow,\
(69) O subduer of pride, (70) O remover of the devotees’ distress!

#### 16

    sudarśana-mochana śrī-śaṅkhachūḍāntaka
    rāmānuja śyāmachā̐da muralī-vādaka

(71) O liberator of (the Vidyādhara) Sudarśan, (72) O destroyer of Śrī Śaṅkhachūḍa, (73) O younger brother of Balarām, (74) O dark moon,\
(75) O player of the muralī flute!

#### 17

    gopī-gīta-śrotā madhusūdana murāri
    ariṣṭa-ghātaka rādhā-kuṇḍādi-vihārī

(76) O hearer of the Gopī-gītā, (77) O destroyer of Madhu,\
(78) O enemy of Mura, (79) O slayer of Ariṣṭa,\
(80) O reveller at Rādhā Kuṇḍa and other places!

#### 18

    vyomāntaka padma-netra keśī-nisūdana
    raṅga-krīḍa kaṁsa-hantā malla-praharaṇa

(81) O killer of Vyoma, (82) O lotus-eyed one, (83) O destroyer of Keśī, (84) O Lord of merry play, (85) O killer of Kaṁsa,\
(86) O combatant of the wrestlers!

#### 19

    vasudeva-suta vṛṣṇi-vaṁśa-kīrti-dhvaja
    dīnanātha mathureśa devakī-garbhaja

(87) O son of Vasudev, (88) O emblem of the Vṛṣṇi dynasty’s fame,\
(89) O Lord of the poor, (90) O Lord of Mathurā,\
(91) O son of Devakī!

#### 20

    kubjā-kṛpāmaya viṣṇu śauri nārāyaṇa
    dvārakeśa naraka-ghna śrī-yadu-nandana

(92) O He who is merciful to Kubjā, (93) O all-pervading one,\
(94) O descendant of Śūrasena, (95) O shelter of all beings,\
(96) O Lord of Dvārakā, (97) O destroyer of Naraka,\
(98) O son of Yadu!

#### 21

    śrī-rukmiṇī-kānta satyā-pati sura-pāla
    pāṇḍava-bāndhava śiśupālādira kāla

(99) O husband of Rukmiṇī, (100) O husband of Satyābhāmā,\
(101) O protector of the gods, (102) O dear friend of the Pāṇḍavas,\
(103) O death of Śiśupāl and others!

#### 22

    jagadīśa janārdana keśavārta-trāṇa
    sarva-avatāra-bīja viśvera nidāna

(104) O Lord of the universe, (105) O exciter of the people,\
(106) O He of fine hair, (107) O deliverer from distress,\
(108) O origin of all Avatārs, (109) O cause of the universe!

#### 23

    māyeśvara yogeśvara brahma-tejādhāra
    sarvātmāra ātmā prabhu prakṛtira pāra

(110) O Lord of Māyā, (111) O master of yoga,\
(112) O source of the Brahman effulgence, (113) O soul of all souls,\
(114) O master of all souls, (115) O far shore of material nature!

#### 24

    patita-pāvana jagannātha sarveśvara
    vṛndāvanachandra sarva-rasera ākara

(116) O purifier of the fallen, (117) O Lord of the universe,\
(118) O Lord of all, (119) O moon of Vṛndāvan,\
(120) O source of all rasa!

#### 25

    nagare nagare gorā gāya
    bhakati-vinoda tachhu pāya

From village to village, Śrī Gaurāṅga sings, and Bhakti Vinod falls at His feet.
