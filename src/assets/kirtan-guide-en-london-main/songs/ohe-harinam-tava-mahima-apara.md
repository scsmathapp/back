# Śrī Nāmāṣṭakam 7

### by Śrīla Bhakti Vinod Ṭhākur

#### 1

    ohe harināma, tava mahimā apāra
    tava pade nati āmi kari bāra bāra

O Holy Name! Your glories are boundless! Therefore I bow down at Your lotus feet again and again.

#### 2

    gokulera mahotsava ānanda-sāgara
    tomāra charaṇe poḍi haiyā kātara

O grand festival of Gokula! O ocean of bliss! I fall down at Your lotus feet, for I am feeling very distressed and troubled at heart.

#### 3

    tumi kṛṣṇa, pūrṇa-vapu, rasera nidāna
    tava pade paḍi tava guṇa kari gāna

You are Lord Kṛṣṇa, Your divine form is fully perfect and complete, and You are the origin of all transcendental mellows. Falling down at Your lotus feet, I sing of Your divine qualities.

#### 4

    je kare tomāra pade ekānta āśraya
    tā’ra ārti-rāśi nāśa karaha niśchaya

You definitely destroy the multitude of afflictions of that person who takes exclusive shelter at Your holy feet.

#### 5

    sarva aparādha tumi nāśa kara tā’ra
    nāma-aparādhāvadhi nāśaho tāhāra

You destroy all of their offences, even to the extent of the Nāma-aparādhas (the ten offences against the chanting of the Holy Name).

#### 6

    sarva-doṣa dhauta kari’ tāhāra hṛdaya
    siṁhāsane baisa tumi parama āśraya

Cleansing them of all impurities, You are then seated upon the throne of their heart as their supreme shelter.

#### 7

    ati-ramya chida-ghana-ānanda-mūrtimāna
    ‘raso vai saḥ’ bali’ veda kare tuyā gāna

You are delightful and beautiful, the personification of complete cognisance and condensed bliss. The Vedas sing of You, saying, “The Supreme Personality of Godhead is verily the personification of all transcendental mellows.”

#### 8

    bhaktivinoda rūpa-gosvāmī-charaṇe
    māgaye sarvadā nāma-sphūrti sarva-kṣane

At the lotus feet of Śrīla Rūpa Goswāmī, Bhakti Vinod constantly begs at every moment for the direct revelation of the Holy Name.
