# Prabhu Kahe – Kahilāṅ ei Mahāmantra

## Śrī Chaitanya-bhāgavata: Madhya-khaṇḍa 23.76–78, 28.27–28

### by Śrīla Vṛndāvan Dās Ṭhākur

    “hare kṛṣṇa hare kṛṣṇa kṛṣṇa kṛṣṇa hare hare
    hare rāma hare rāma rāma rāma hare hare”

    prabhu kahe – “kahilāṅ ei mahāmantra
    ihā japa’ giyā sabe kariyā nirbandha

    ihā haite sarva-siddhi ha-ibe sabāra
    sarva-kṣaṇa bala’ ithe vidhi nāhi āra

    ki śayane ki bhojane, ki vā jāgaraṇe
    ahar-niśa chinta kṛṣṇa, balaha vadane

    āma-prati sneha yadi thāke sabākāra
    kṛṣṇa vinā keha kichhu nā balibe āra”

Śrīman Mahāprabhu said, “Hare Kṛṣṇa Hare Kṛṣṇa, Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa Hare Hare, Hare Rāma Hare Rāma, Rāma Rāma Hare Hare.

“I have told you this Mahāmantra. All of you chant it with determination and without fail. By doing so, everyone will attain all spiritual perfection.

“At all times chant this Mahāmantra; there are no other rules. Whether you are resting, eating, or awake – day and night think of Kṛṣṇa and chant His Name aloud.

“If you have any affection for Me, then do not speak of anyone or any­thing other than Kṛṣṇa.”
