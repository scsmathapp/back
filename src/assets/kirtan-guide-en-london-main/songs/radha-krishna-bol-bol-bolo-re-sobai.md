# Śrī Nagara Kīrtana 4

## Śrī Nāma from Gītāvalī

### by Śrīla Bhakti Vinod Ṭhākur

#### 1

                    ‘rādhā-kṛṣṇa’ bal bal balare sabāi
            (ei) śikṣā diyā, saba nadīyā
                    phirchhe neche’ gaura-nitāi
    (michhe) māyāra baśe, jāchchha bhese,
                    khāchchha hābuḍubu, bhāi

Chant, chant “Rādhā-Kṛṣṇa!” Everyone chant! When Lord Chaitanya and Lord Nityānanda came dancing through Nadia, They gave these teachings: Chant, chant “Rādhā-Kṛṣṇa!” Everyone chant! You are caught up in a whirlpool of senseless action and are sinking lower and lower. Chant, chant “Rādhā-Kṛṣṇa!” Everyone chant!

#### 2

            (jīva) kṛṣṇa-dāsa, e biśvāsa,
                    karle ta āra duḥkha nāi
    (kṛṣṇa) balbe jabe, pulaka habe,
                    jharabe āṅkhi bali tāi

If you just understand that the spirit soul is the eternal servant of Kṛṣṇa, you will never have any more sorrows. Chant Hare Kṛṣṇa and your eyes will fill with tears and your body will feel transcendental shivering. Chant, chant “Rādhā-Kṛṣṇa!” Everyone chant!

#### 3

    (rādhā) kṛṣṇa bala, saṅge chala,
                    ei-mātra bhikhā chāi
            (jāy) sakala bipada bhaktivinoda,
                    bolena jakhana o-nāma gāi

Simply chant “Rādhā-Kṛṣṇa!” and join with us. Those are the only alms we beg. Chant, chant “Rādhā-Kṛṣṇa!” Everyone chant! “All dangers will be gone when that Name is chanted,” says Bhakti Vinod Ṭhākur. Chant, chant “Rādhā-Kṛṣṇa!” Everyone chant!
