# Śrī Govinda Kuṇḍa Māhātmya

## The Glories of Śrī Govinda Kuṇḍa

### Sanskrit verses from Śrī Girirāj-khaṇḍam, Śrī Garga-saṁhitā.

    atha govardhane ramye surabhir gauḥ samudra-jā
    snāpayām āsa gopeśaṁ dugdha-dhārābhir ātmanaḥ
    surabher dugdha-dhārābhir govinda-snānato nṛpa
    jāto govinda-kuṇḍo ’drau mahā-pāpa-haraḥ paraḥ
    kadāchit tasmin dugdhasya svādutvaṁ pratipadyate
    tatra snātvā naraḥ sākṣād govinda-padam āpnuyāt
    govinda-kuṇḍe viśade yaḥ snāti kṛṣṇa-mānasaḥ
    prāpnoti kṛṣṇa-sārūpyaṁ maithilendra na saṁśayaḥ

*Śrīla Bhakti Sundar Govinda Dev-Goswāmī Mahārāj’s corresponding Bengali composition:*

#### 1

    samudra-sambhavā gābhī surabhī āpana
    dugdhe abhiṣeka kaila vrajendra-nandana

Surabhī, the divine cow who appeared from churning the milk ocean, lovingly bathed Vrajendra Nandan Kṛṣṇa with her own milk.

#### 2

    sei dugdhe pūrṇa kuṇḍa ‘śrī govinda’ nāma
    ramya giri govardhane śobhe abhirāma

The pond filled with that milk, ‘Śrī Govinda Kuṇḍa’, graces beautiful Govardhan with exquisite beauty.

#### 3

    kuṇḍabāri mahāpāpa-hārī se chinmaya
    snāne pāne bhava-bhaya tritāpa nāśaya

The transcendental water of this kuṇḍa can banish even the greatest sins. The threefold miseries – ādhyātmik, ādhidaivik, and ādhibhautik – and all fears of this material world are destroyed by bathing in or drinking this water.

#### 4

    bahu-bhāgye keha sei dugdhāsvāda pāya
    chidānanda-deha labhi kṛṣṇa-loke yāya

Sometimes, by great fortune, someone will get to taste the water of Govinda Kuṇḍa as that milk, and, attaining a transcendentally joyful form, go to Kṛṣṇaloka.

#### 5

    śrī kṛṣṇa-mānase yebā kare hethā snāna
    gupta-govardhane rādhā-kṛṣṇa-sevā pāna

Anyone who bathes in Śrī Govinda Kuṇḍa desiring to serve Kṛṣṇa, attains the divine service of Śrī Śrī Rādhā-Kṛṣṇa in Gupta Govardhan (hidden Govardhan).
