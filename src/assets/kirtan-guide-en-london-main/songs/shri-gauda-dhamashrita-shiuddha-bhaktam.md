# Śrīla Gaura Kiśor Dās Bābājī Mahārāj Aṣṭakam

## Eight Verses in Obeisance to Śrīla Gaura Kiśor Dās Bābājī Mahārāj

### Sanskrit composition
### by Śrīla Bhakti Rakṣak Śrīdhar Dev-Goswāmī Mahārāj

#### 1

    śrī-gauḍa-dhāmāśrita-śuddha-bhaktaṁ
    rūpānugādyaṁ niravady arūpam
    vairāgya-dharmojjvala-vigrahaṁ taṁ
    vande prabhuṁ gaura-kiśora-saṁjñam

A pure devotee sheltered in the holy land of Gauḍa, the best of the followers of Śrī Rūpa, he has a faultless ascetic form, and is an effulgent embodiment of renunciation. I offer obeisances to him, the master Gaura Kiśor.

#### 2

    asat-prasaṅgaṁ parihāya nityaṁ
    gaurāṅga-sevā-vrata-magna-chittam
    gauḍa-vrajābheda-viśiṣṭa-prajñaṁ
    vande prabhuṁ gaura-kiśora-saṁjñam

Rejecting all bad association, his heart is ever immersed in the vow of service to Śrī Gaurāṅga, and he is filled with realisation of the non-difference of Gauḍa and Vraja. I offer obeisances to the master Gaura Kiśor.

#### 3

    śrī-dhāma-māyāpura-divya-gūḍha-
    māhātmya-gītonmukhaṁ vareṇyam
    dhanyaṁ mahābhāgavatāgragaṇyaṁ
    vande prabhuṁ gaura-kiśora-saṁjñam

Intent upon chanting the secret divine glories of Śrī Dhām Māyāpur, he is exalted, admirable, and the foremost of advanced devotees. I offer obeisances to the master Gaura Kiśor.

#### 4

    pūtāvadhūta-vraja-śīrṣa-ratnaṁ
    śrī-rādhikā-kṛṣṇa-nigūḍha-bhaktam
    sadā vrajāveśa-sarāga-cheṣṭaṁ
    vande prabhuṁ gaura-kiśora-saṁjñam

The crown jewel of the pure mendicants and the residents of Vraja, he is a confidential devotee of Śrī Śrī Rādhā-Kṛṣṇa and in the ecstatic mood of Vraja is ever engaged in rāgānugā-bhakti. I offer obeisances to the master Gaura Kiśor.

#### 5

    śokāspadātīta-prabhāva-ramyaṁ
    mūḍhair avedyaṁ praṇatābhigamyam
    nityānubhūtāchyuta-sad-vilāsaṁ
    vande prabhuṁ gaura-kiśora-saṁjñam

He has a delightful splendour beyond the reach of sorrow. Incom­prehensible to the ignorant, but attainable by the surrendered, he constantly tastes the infallible Lord’s eternal Pastimes. I offer obeisances to the master Gaura Kiśor.

#### 6

    kāpaṭya-dharmānvita-chaṇḍa-daṇḍa-
    vidhāyakaṁ sajjana-saṅga-raṅgam
    śrī-kṛṣṇa-chaitanya-padābja-bhṛṅgaṁ
    vande prabhuṁ gaura-kiśora-saṁjñam

He is a vehement subduer of the deceitful, is cheerful in the association of the sincere, and is a bee in the lotus feet of Śrī Kṛṣṇa Chaitanya. I offer obeisances to the master Gaura Kiśor.

#### 7

    dāmodarotthāna-dine pradhāne
    kṣetre pavitre kuliyābhidhāne
    prapañcha-līlā-parihāravantaṁ
    vande prabhuṁ gaura-kiśora-saṁjñam

In the month of Dāmodar, on the principal day of Utthān Ekādaśī, in the holy place known as Kuliyā, he withdrew his manifest Pastimes. I offer obeisances to the master Gaura Kiśor.

#### 8

    tava hi “dayita-dāse” satya-sūrya-prakāśe
    jagati durita-nāśe prodyate chid-vilāse
    vayam anugata-bhṛtyāḥ pāda-padmaṁ prapannā
    anudinam anukampāṁ prārthayāmo nagaṇyāḥ

Manifest in your Dayita Dās as the sun of truth to destroy the sin in this world and manifest the Lord’s spiritual Pastimes, we, his insignificant followers and servants, take shelter at your lotus feet and pray continuously for your mercy.
