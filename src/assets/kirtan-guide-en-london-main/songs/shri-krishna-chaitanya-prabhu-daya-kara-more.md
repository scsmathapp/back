# Sāvaraṇa Śrī-Gaura-pāda-padme Prārthanā

### Verses 1 to 7 by Śrīla Narottam Dās Ṭhākur

#### 1

    śrī-kṛṣṇa-chaitanya prabhu dayā kara more
    tomā vinā ke dayālu jagata mājhāre

Lord Śrī Kṛṣṇa Chaitanya Mahāprabhu, kindly give Your grace to me; in the whole creation, there’s none as merciful as You.

#### 2

    patita-pāvana-hetu tava avatāra
    mo-sama patita prabhu nā pāibe āra

You descended just to save the fallen souls; O Lord, a soul as fallen as me is nowhere to be found.

#### 3

    hā hā prabhu nityānanda premānanda sukhī
    kṛpāvalokana kara āmi baḍa duḥkhī

O Lord, O Lord Nityānanda ever in the joy of Your love; kindly give Your gracious glance, I am very sad.

#### 4

    dayā kara sītā-pati advaita gosāñi
    tava kṛpā-bale pāi chaitanya-nitāi

Kindly give Your grace, Śrī Advaita Goswāmī, the Lord of Śrīmatī Sītā Ṭhākurāṇī; by the power of Your grace we may gain the service of Śrī Śrī Nitāi-Chaitanya.

#### 5

    gaura-premamaya-tanu paṇḍit gadādhara
    śrīnivāsa haridāsa dayāra sāgara

The embodiment of Śrī Gaura’s love, Paṇḍit Gadādhar; Śrīvās Paṇḍit, Haridās Ṭhākur, you are the ocean of grace personified.

#### 6

    hā hā svarūpa sanātana rūpa raghunātha
    bhaṭṭa-yuga śrī-jīva hā prabhu lokanātha

O my Lords, Śrī Svarūp Dāmodar, Śrī Sanātan, Śrī Rūpa and Śrī Raghunāth Dās, Śrī Raghunāth Bhaṭṭa and Śrī Gopāl Bhaṭṭa. O my Lord Śrī Lokanāth Goswāmī.

#### 7

    dayā kara śrī-āchārya prabhu śrīnivāsa
    rāmachandra-saṅga māge narottama dāsa

Please grant Your blessings O Lord Śrī Śrīnivās Āchārya, for the company of Śrī Rāmachandra Chakravartī, Narottam Dās does pray.

    dayā kara prabhupāda śrī-gaura-prakāśa
    tava jana-kṛpā māge ei adhama dāsa

Kindly give Your grace, Śrīla Prabhupād Saraswatī Ṭhākur, manifestation of Śrī Gaura. This fallen servant begs the mercy of your associates. *(Verse by Śrīla Bhakti Rakṣak Śrīdhar Dev-Goswāmī Mahārāj)*
