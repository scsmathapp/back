# Śrī Śikṣāṣṭakam

## Verse Three

### by Śrī Chaitanya Mahāprabhu in Sanskrit

    tṛṇād api sunīchena taror iva sahiṣṇunā
    amāninā mānadena kīrtanīyaḥ sadā hariḥ

One who is humbler than a blade of grass, more forbearing than a tree, who gives due honour to others without desiring it for himself is qualified to always chant the Holy Name of Kṛṣṇa.

*Śrīla Bhakti Vinod Ṭhākur’s corresponding Bengali composition:*

#### 1

    śrī-kṛṣṇa-kīrtane yadi mānasa tohāra
    parama yatane ta̐hi labha adhikāra

If your mind is always carefully absorbed in the chanting of the Holy Name, then you will become qualified for chanting the Holy Name of Kṛṣṇa.

#### 2

    tṛṇādhika hīna dīna akiñchana chhāra
    āpane mānabi sadā chhāḍi’ ahaṅkāra

Humbler than a blade of grass, considering yourself low, poor, and meek, give up your false ego.

#### 3

    vṛkṣa-sama kṣamā-guṇa karabi sādhana
    pratihiṁsā tyaji’ anye karabi pālana

Tolerant as a tree, you should forgive and forbear, giving up retaliation. Practise nurturing and protecting others.

#### 4

    jīvana-nirvāhe āne udvega nā dibe
    para-upakāre nija-sukha pāsaribe

Live without causing anxiety to others; do good unto others without considering your own happiness.

#### 5

    ha-ileo sarva-guṇe guṇī mahāśaya
    pratiṣṭhāśā chhāḍi’ kara amānī hṛdaya

A great soul who has all these qualities should not become proud of his saintly qualities, but, giving up position and prestige, he should always remain prideless within his heart.

#### 6

    kṛṣṇa-adhiṣṭhāna sarva-jīve jāni’ sadā
    karabi sammāna sabe ādare sarvadā

Always knowing that Kṛṣṇa dwells within the hearts of all living beings, you should always properly respect and honour others.

#### 7

    dainya, dayā, anye māna, pratiṣṭhā-varjana
    chāri guṇe guṇī ha-i’ karaha kīrtana

Humility, mercy, consideration of others, and giving up position are the four qualities needed to perform kīrtan.

#### 8

    bhakati-vinoda kā̐di’ bale prabhu-pāya
    hena adhikāra kabe dibe he amāya

Bhakti Vinod, crying at the Lord’s holy feet says, “O my Lord, when will You bestow upon me the qualifications for Kṛṣṇa-kīrtan?”
