# Śrī Śikṣāṣṭakam

## Verse Eight (b)

*Second related composition by Śrīla Bhakti Vinod Ṭhākur:*

(Regarding this, Śrīla Śrīdhar Mahārāj advised not to sing this composition because “it is too high.”)

#### 1

    yoga-pīṭhopari-sthita    aṣṭa-sakhī-suveṣṭita
        vṛndāraṇye kadamba-kānane
    rādhā-saha vaṁśī-dhārī    viśva-jana-chitta-hārī
        prāṇa mora tā̐hāra charaṇe

At the yoga-pīṭha, surrounded by the aṣṭa-sakhīs, in Vṛndāvan Forest, in a grove of kadamba trees, by the side of Śrī Rādhā, holding His flute, Śrī Kṛṣṇa enchants all souls within the universe. I fall at His feet and surrender my life to Him.

#### 2

    sakhī-ājñāmata kari do̐hāra sevana
    pālya-dāsī sadā bhāvi do̐hāra charaṇa

Following the orders of a sakhī, I engage in service to the holy pair, Śrī Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa, always considering myself a dependent maidservant at Their lotus feet.

#### 3

    kabhu kṛpā kari’    mama hasta dhari’
        madhura-vachana bale
    tāmbula la-iyā    khāya dui jane
        mālā laya kutūhale

Sometimes being kind, They take me by the hand and speak sweet words to me out of affection. Taking the betel nuts I have prepared, the two of Them eat and accept a garland from me with great delight.

#### 4

    adarśana haya kakhana ki chhale
    nā dekhiyā do̐he hiyā mora jvale

Again, They disappear from my vision by some trick. Not seeing Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa, my heart burns in agony.

#### 5

    yekhāne sekhāne    thākuka du’jane
        āmi ta charaṇa-dāsī
    milane ānanda    virahe yātanā
        sakala samāna vāsi

Here or there, wherever They stay, I am the maidservant of Their lotus feet. Through the happiness of union and the agony of separation, I am Their maidservant.

#### 6

    rādhā-kṛṣṇa prāṇa mora jīvane maraṇe
    more rākhi’ māri’ sukhe thākuka du’jane

Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa are my life and soul in life and in death; whether They protect me or take my life, may They always be happy.

#### 7

    bhakati-vinoda    āna nāhi jāne
        paḍi’ nija sakhī-pāya
    rādhikāra gaṇe    thākiyā satata
        yugala-charaṇa chāya

Bhakti Vinoda says: “I know nothing else. Falling at the feet of my own sakhī (confidante), remaining always among the associates of Rādhikā, I take shelter in the shade of the Divine Couple’s feet.”
